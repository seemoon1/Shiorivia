# Shiorivia Brand & Marks Policy / Shiorivia 品牌标识使用政策

**Status / 状态:** Community policy draft v0.1 (2026-10-29). This is a public permission framework for brand-related assets, **not** a statement that any specific mark is already registered. Its legal scope depends on ownership and applicable law. / 社区政策初稿，不宣称特定商标已经注册；权利范围以实际权属及法律为准。

## 1. Separate the code from the brand / 源码与品牌分离

Shiorivia's **software source code** is licensed under [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). AGPL permits commercial use of the code, subject to its terms. It does **not** automatically grant a right to present a third-party product, service or company as an **official Shiorivia** offering.

Shiorivia's name, wordmarks, logos, star-road symbols, official icons, branding graphics, and similar identifying assets are subject to this separate policy **to the extent rights are held and enforceable**. Third-party marks and content retain their own rights and are not covered by these permissions.

Shiorivia 软件源码按 AGPL-3.0 授权，遵守协议即可商用；**源码授权不等于取得 Shiorivia 官方品牌的商业使用权**。名称、文字标识、Logo、星轨图案及官方视觉资产在权利依法存在的范围内适用本独立政策。其他版权方的素材不包含在本授权内。

## 2. Allowed without asking / 无需申请的使用

You may use Shiorivia's name and unaltered official logo in **noncommercial** community contexts, such as tutorials, reviews, fan communities, open-source integrations, presentations, free plugins and educational examples, provided that you:

1. Clearly identify your project or account as **unofficial / non-official** wherever a viewer might mistake it for the official project;
2. Do not imply sponsorship, partnership, endorsement, certification or official status;
3. Do not register a confusing domain name, organization, product name, application icon or social account using the marks as its primary identity;
4. Do not modify the official logo into a confusing variant, or obscure its origin; and
5. Credit the Shiorivia project where reasonably practicable, linking to the official project or source.

**Noncommercial community activity** includes genuinely free tutorials, non-monetized fan pages and community demo plugins. Incidental discussion of Shiorivia on commercial news sites, product compatibility statements, legally protected nominative/fair use and required attribution are not prohibited simply because the surrounding context is commercial.

允许在**非商业**教程、评测、社区交流、免费插件、教学和演示中合理使用 Shiorivia 名称和未经修改的官方 Logo，但应：**清楚标注「非官方」**；不得声称官方授权或合作；不得以官方品牌充当第三方产品、域名、组织或账号的主要身份；不得制作易造成混淆的 Logo 变体；适当注明来源。

合理、真实的兼容性介绍、依法受保护的指称性使用或必须保留的版权/许可证声明，不会仅因发生在商业环境就被本政策一律禁止。

### Example disclaimer / 示例声明

> Unofficial community project. Not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Shiorivia project.
> 非官方社区项目，与 Shiorivia 官方无隶属、赞助或背书关系。

## 3. Written permission required / 需要事先书面授权

- Using Shiorivia marks as the brand for **paid hosting, commercial products, paid support services, merchandising, advertisements**, or commercial partnerships;
- Selling merchandise or products carrying official logos or branding;
- Using the official mark as the principal identity of a derivative application or commercial service;
- Claiming to be official, a certified partner, or an authorized representative.

对**收费托管、商业产品、付费支持、周边商品、广告、商业合作**使用 Shiorivia 品牌标识，或者把官方标识作为第三方产品主要品牌的，须先取得权利人书面许可。即使免费，冒充官方也不被允许。

**Important:** This section governs the use of marks and branding, **not** the use, sale, distribution or modification of AGPL-licensed code. A third party may offer a paid service based on the AGPL code under its **own independent branding**, subject to the AGPL and other applicable requirements.

## 4. Use in forks / 分支与衍生项目

A fork may truthfully say it is “based on Shiorivia” and retain notices required by the software license. If modified into an independently distributed app or service, choose a distinguishing name, icon and public-facing branding. Avoid using official Shiorivia marks in ways that confuse users about provenance or official support.

Fork 可以如实标注「基于 Shiorivia」，并应保留源码协议要求的声明；但作为独立产品对外发布时，请采用易于识别的独立名称与 Logo，避免造成官方出品的误解。

## 5. Brand assets, permissions, and contact / 资产、许可与联系

Official brand asset files are **not automatically relicensed under AGPL merely because they are distributed alongside code**. Their copying/reuse is governed by this policy and applicable law, except where an individual asset carries an explicit different license. The policy cannot override rights of third parties or rights granted by law. A draft or prior version of a logo is not necessarily protected as copyright or a registered trademark in all places.

For commercial branding permissions, open a **non-public contact channel** specified by the Shiorivia project maintainers before using the mark. Until a contact address is published, do **not** submit confidential business terms or secrets to public Issues. No automatic approval is implied.

官方品牌素材不因与代码放在同一仓库就自动变成 AGPL 代码；素材的复制和展示由本政策、具体素材授权及适用法律决定。尚未公开专门的商标授权邮箱时，请等待项目维护者提供私密联系渠道，不要在公开 Issue 中提交商业机密。

*Policy steward / 政策管理者：Shiorivia project maintainer(s)（项目维护者）。这不是关于商标注册状态、专有权来源或法律执行效果的保证；正式对外推广前建议由相关司法辖区律师审阅。*
